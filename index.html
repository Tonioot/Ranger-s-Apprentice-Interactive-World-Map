    The known world

    Every place, country and sea on this map, with the books they appear in. The map itself is interactive — this list is the same content as plain text.

    Countries and regions

    Alpina

    The high mountain range that separates the north from the south. The passes freeze shut in winter; anyone wanting to go south from Gallica then must wait for spring or sail around.

    Araluen

    The kingdom of King Duncan, divided into fifty fiefs each ruled by a baron. Its defense rests on three pillars: the barons' armies, the Battleschools that train knights, and the Ranger Corps — fifty scouts with a mottled cloak, a double quiver, and a reputation far bigger than their numbers.

    Arrida

    The desert land south of the Constant Sea, ruled by an emir with wakirs as governors. Order and trade rule in the cities; beyond them the Bedullin nomads and the feared Tualaghi hold sway. Anyone who enters the desert without a guide or a sworn friend does not come out.

    Aslava

    A vast land east of Teutlandt and Alpina, merging into the plains the Temujai come from. Anyone traveling east by land passes through here.

    Baralat

    The land at the eastern end of the Constant Sea, bordering Arrida. Just inside that border lies the Amrashin massif, close to the mouth of the Assaranyan Canal.

    Celtica

    The mountainous southwest of the island, bound to Araluen by a mutual defense treaty. The Celts are miners and stonemasons, not soldiers — when Morgarath emptied their villages to build his bridge, they could do little to stop him.

    Eastern Steppes

    The plains in the far east, beyond Aslava: the land of the Temujai. They fight on horseback and with the bow, and swept west from here until they were stopped in Skandia.

    Gallica

    South of the Stormwhite Sea. There is a king in name, but power lies with warlords who each hold their own castle, road, and toll. For travelers, Gallica is therefore the most dangerous land in the west — and the standard escape route for anyone fleeing Araluen.

    Helleno

    The land in the northeast of the Constant Sea. In the books it isn't a continuous coastline but a remote island group. Many Hellenics live by piracy; they stand out with brightly colored clothes and jewelry that clashes wildly. They worship the goddess Ariadne.

    Hibernia

    The island west of Araluen, split into six kingdoms that would rather distrust each other than cooperate. That very division is what the Outsiders cult exploited, kingdom after kingdom. Halt was born here, in Clonmel.

    Iberion

    The peninsula in the southwest. Anyone sailing south from the north toward Arrida or the Constant Sea rounds this coast first.

    Indus

    The land in the far east, between the western kingdoms and Nihon-Ja. Anyone sailing east passes this coast.

    Middle Kingdoms

    The kingdoms between Ursali and the east. When the Temujai first swept west, they were overrun early, together with Ursali.

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The inhospitable mountain range in the south, behind Morgarath's Plateau. Morgarath withdrew here after his defeat, with his army of Wargals — beast-like warriors who receive his will directly from his thoughts. The region is cut off from Araluen by the Fissure; the only way through is Three Step Pass.

    Nihon-Ja

    The island empire in the far east, half a year's sailing from Araluen. The land is ruled by Emperor Shigeru and defended by the Senshi, a warrior class that fights with the long sword and holds to a strict code of honor. When the Senshi lord Arisaka seizes power, the emperor flees into the mountains, to the hidden fortress of Ran-Koshi — where the Kikori, woodcutters with no fighting experience, learn to fight in close formation.

    Picta

    Rugged highlands in the north of the island, the land of the Scotti clans. They raid the northern fiefs whenever the border strongholds weaken — hence the ring of castles of which Macindaw is one.

    Skandia

    The northern seafaring land on the Stormwhite Sea. Skandians sail wolfships, fight with the heavy battleaxe, and long lived off raiding — until the treaty with Araluen, forged after the joint fight against the Temujai, made them allies. The Oberjarl stands at their head.

    Sonderland

    The forested land west of Skandia, on the passage between the open ocean and the Stormwhite Sea. Anyone sailing from Araluen to Hallasholm passes this way.

    Teutlandt

    The land east of the Stormwhite Sea, which Halt and Horace crossed on their way to the Skandian coast.

    Toscana

    A southern land of city-states, traders and marble, famous for its legions: professional soldiers who fight shield to shield in close formation. That style of fighting is held up as the standard across the known world.

    Ursali

    The land at the center of the continent, between Aslava and Alpina in the west and the Eastern Steppes in the east. The Ursali kept only a small army, and when the Temujai swept west their land was one of the first to fall — together with the Middle Kingdoms. What was told of their defeat traveled ahead of the Temujai.

    Places on the map

    Al Shabah

    Village or town · Arrida

    The city of Wakir Selethen, the Arridi commander who first opposes and then allies with Halt in the hunt for the Tualaghi.

    Erak's Ransom

    Bayrath

    Village or town · Toscana

    A large city, almost a metropolis, on the eastern bank of the Dan. The corrupt mayor (the Gatmeister) was bribed by Zavac: the Herons were imprisoned and their ship seized, and Lydia narrowly escaped being sold into slavery.

    The Hunters

    Byzantos

    Harbour town · Toscana

    A young, energetic city-state on the Golden Reach, founded by Constantus the First when the Toscan Empire grew too large for one ruler. Protected on three sides by water and secured behind thick walls. Here the Herons met Empress Justina.

    The Caldera

    Caraway

    Castle · Araluen

    A fief in the northeast, with its own Battleschool.

    The Royal Ranger, Duel at Araluen

    Castle Araluen

    Castle · Araluen

    The royal stronghold: slender white towers, sprawling gardens, and the seat of King Duncan. Princess Cassandra grows up here — the girl Will knew in Skandia as Evanlyn. In the Royal Ranger books the castle is besieged by the Red Fox Clan.

    The Ruins of Gorlan, The Burning Bridge, Erak's Ransom, The Royal Ranger, The Red Fox Clan, Duel at Araluen

    Castle Macindaw

    Castle · Araluen

    A border stronghold on the edge of Picta, built to keep the Scotti out. When the castle fell into enemy hands through treachery, Will infiltrated it as the traveling juggler Will Barton, and recaptured it with the help of Malcolm, Alyss and the Skandian Gundar.

    The Sorcerer of the North, The Siege of Macindaw

    Castle Redmont

    Castle · Araluen

    The red castle of Baron Arald, and Will's home. Redmont has a Battleschool under Battlemaster Rodney, a kitchen ruled by Master Chubb, and a courier service under Lady Pauline. Just outside the walls, at the edge of the forest, stands the Ranger's cabin: here Halt takes the orphan Will as his apprentice.

    The Ruins of Gorlan, The Burning Bridge, Erak's Ransom, Halt's Peril, The Lost Stories, The Royal Ranger

    Château La Lumière

    Castle · Gallica

    The Gallican capital and seat of King Philippe, who deposed the weak King Henri nine years earlier. Despite its elegant name it is just as heavily fortified as Montsombre and Falaises. From here Philippe travels to Castle Araluen to beg King Duncan to free his son Giles.

    The Missing Prince, Escape from Falaise

    Château Montsombre

    Castle · Gallica

    The black castle of the warlord Deparnieux, who left his prisoners to die in cages along the road. He held Halt and Horace as 'guests' for months, until Horace defeated him in single combat — and left the castle to Halt, who passed it on to its people.

    The Icebound Land

    Château des Falaises

    Castle · Gallica

    The coastal stronghold of Baron Joubert de Lassigny, who holds Prince Giles captive and conspires against King Philippe with his brother Louis. Will and Maddie infiltrate it as jongleurs but are unmasked and imprisoned; in the next book they manage to escape and free the prince after all.

    The Missing Prince, Escape from Falaise

    Craikennis

    Village or town · Hibernia

    A village in Clonmel that Halt, Will and Horace helped defend against a band of raiders. The attack had been staged by the Outsiders cult, who terrorize villages first so they can sell them 'protection' afterward.

    The Kings of Clonmel

    Cresthaven

    Harbour town · Araluen

    The bay given to Hal Mikkelson and the crew of the Heron as their home port when they were sent to Araluen as a 'duty ship' — a Skandian vessel that, under the treaty, patrols the Araluen coast for a year.

    Slaves of Socorro

    Dan River

    River · Toscana

    A great river that cuts through Toscana from north to south, from the Stormwhite coast to near the Constant Sea. Along its banks, from source to mouth, lie Krall, Bayrath and Raguza. Here the Herons pursued the pirate Zavac after the attack on Limmat, bypassing a dangerous set of rapids via a side channel along the way.

    The Hunters

    Dun Kilty

    Castle · Hibernia

    The capital of Clonmel, one of the six kingdoms of Hibernia. King Ferris rules here — Halt's twin brother. Halt was the elder and thus heir to the throne, but gave up his claim after Ferris twice tried to have him killed.

    The Kings of Clonmel

    East Cliffs

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The eastern cliff edge of the mountains, above the sea.

    Genovesa

    Village or town · Toscana

    An independent Toscan city-state, notorious for its assassins: master knife-fighters who carry multiple blades. In Halt's Peril, Tennyson hires Genovesan assassins to eliminate Halt, Will and Horace; in Ambush at Sorato, Will instead recruits Genovesan crossbowmen for the fight against the Temujai. The city itself never appears on-page, only its hired hands.

    Halt's Peril, Ambush at Sorato

    Gorlan

    Ruin · Araluen

    Once Morgarath's fief, until he rose in rebellion against the crown. Since his defeat the castle lies in ruins and the local farmers avoid the area. Fifteen years earlier, Morgarath's first bid for power also began on the tournament field at Gorlan.

    The Ruins of Gorlan, The Tournament at Gorlan

    Grimsdell Wood

    Araluen

    A misty, twisted wood near Macindaw, where the villagers fear the sorcerer Malkallam. In truth that's Malcolm: a brilliant healer who uses light, smoke and sound to impress people into leaving him alone. His knowledge of herbs and poison later saves Halt's life.

    The Sorcerer of the North, The Siege of Macindaw, Halt's Peril

    Hackham Heath

    Battlefield · Araluen

    The battlefield of the first war against Morgarath, fifteen years before Will's apprenticeship. Will's father Daniel died here in an attack that saved Halt's life — the reason Halt later kept an eye on the orphaned boy.

    The Battle of Hackham Heath

    Hallasholm

    Harbour town · Skandia

    The capital of Skandia on the Stormwhite Sea: a wooden Great Hall, a harbor full of wolfships, and a beach where Brotherband training begins every summer — three crews of boys competing for the right to sail as warriors. Here the smallest crew, Hal Mikkelson's Herons, comes into its own; here Will and Evanlyn were once held as slaves.

    The Icebound Land, The Battle for Skandia, The Outcasts, Return of the Temujai

    Ito

    Castle · Nihon-Ja

    The capital of Nihon-Ja and the seat of Emperor Shigeru, with the great winter palace where the court spends the cold season. This is where the rebellion begins: the Senshi lord Arisaka rises up because the emperor means to grant the lower classes — the Kikori woodcutters chief among them — the same rights as the warrior class. Shigeru is forced to flee Ito and heads into the mountains with a small household, making for Ran-Koshi.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Iwanai

    Harbour town · Nihon-Ja

    The port town where Halt, Will, Evanlyn and Alyss came ashore in search of Horace, who had become caught up in the civil war in Nihon-Ja.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Kawagishi

    Harbour town · Nihon-Ja

    A fishing village on the northeastern coast, where the boats are hauled up onto the beach and timber from the mountains is shipped out. It lies on the road Emperor Shigeru and his party follow when, after the fall of Ito, they travel north along the coast in search of the hidden fortress of Ran-Koshi. For the Kikori, Kawagishi is where their timber goes out to sea.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Krall

    Harbour town · Toscana

    A settlement on the northern upper reaches of the Dan, where the Herons put in for supplies during the chase after Zavac. An informant told them about Zavac's route here, but was overheard; their prisoner Rikard was murdered and Hal had to prove the Herons weren't responsible.

    The Hunters

    La Rivage

    Harbour town · Gallica

    The Gallican port town where Halt and Horace came ashore on their journey to Skandia to free Will. Gallica has no strong king: the land is divided among warlords who each tax their own stretch of road and river.

    The Icebound Land

    Land of the Mawagansett

    Far across the Endless Ocean, a storm drove the Heron to a wooded, unknown shore. The crew was taken in by the Mawagansett — a hospitable fishing people raided every year by the dreaded Ghostfaces.

    The Ghostfaces

    Les Sourges

    Village or town · Gallica

    A river town near La Rivage, with a wooden bridge and a ferry that runs most of the year. Will and Maddie pass through here as jongleurs on their way into the Gallican interior.

    The Missing Prince

    Limmat

    Harbour town · Skandia

    A prosperous trading town that lives off its emerald mine. The pirate Zavac seized the town with his ship the Raven; the Herons helped organize its defense and drove him off.

    The Invaders

    Maashava

    Village or town · Arrida

    The walled city of the Tualaghi, the blue-veiled desert raiders everyone fears. Erak was held prisoner here, and here Halt, Will, Horace, Evanlyn and Selethen freed him.

    Erak's Ransom

    Mararoc

    Village or town · Arrida

    The capital of Arrida, seat of the emir, where negotiations over Erak's ransom begin — until it turns out the Oberjarl is no longer in the hands of the people being negotiated with.

    Erak's Ransom

    Mizu Umi Bakudai

    Landscape · Nihon-Ja

    A large mountain lake in northern Nihon-Ja, its name reading as “the vast lake”. The water lies among the wooded slopes where the Kikori cut their timber, on the route the emperor's party follows to the hidden fortress of Ran-Koshi. In winter the shores freeze over and ice drifts on the lake.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Morgarath's Plateau

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The bare plateau before the Mountains of Rain and Night, where Morgarath massed his Wargals.

    The Ruins of Gorlan, The Burning Bridge

    Mountshannon

    Ruin · Hibernia

    The village that didn't pay. The Outsiders' bandits left nothing standing — proof that behind Prophet Tennyson's pious talk lies plain extortion and murder.

    The Kings of Clonmel

    Norgate

    Castle · Araluen

    The northern fief that Macindaw belongs to, and the castle that guards the way into Picta.

    The Sorcerer of the North, The Siege of Macindaw

    North Cliffs

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The steep northern edge of Morgarath's territory, where the plateau drops away toward Araluen.

    Northern Mountains

    Nihon-Ja

    The high range that covers almost the whole north of Nihon-Ja: steep walls, narrow valleys and peaks that lie under snow the year round. The Kikori live on the wooded slopes, making their living from timber; higher still the Hasanu, a taciturn mountain people the Senshi dismiss as barely human, but who know the passes better than anyone. Ran-Koshi lies hidden somewhere in these mountains, and it is the winter freezing the passes shut that gives Shigeru's followers time to turn woodcutters into an army.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Palladio

    Harbour town · Toscana

    A large coastal city in southern Toscana, on the Constant Sea. Palladio itself never appears in the story; the name only surfaces in a song Crowley and Halt quote ("A blacksmith from Palladio, he met a lovely lady").

    Plains of Uthal

    Battlefield · Araluen

    The open field where the Araluen army met the Wargals pouring down from the mountains. When Morgarath demanded single combat, Horace took up the challenge — and killed him.

    The Burning Bridge

    Raguza

    Harbour town · Toscana

    A lawless port town at the southern end of the Dan, ruled by a council of pirate captains (the Korpal): anyone who brings in enough plunder and keeps the house rules is welcome. Ships pay a ten percent toll here. Here the Herons finally tracked Zavac down.

    The Hunters

    Ran-Koshi

    Castle · Nihon-Ja

    A forgotten fortress in a mountain valley, with a narrow approach between steep rock walls. Shigeru's followers dig in here while Horace and Will forge the Kikori woodcutters into an army that learns to fight in close formation.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Rovo

    Ruin · Toscana

    The capital of the ancient Rovo empire, the predecessor civilization of Toscana, which reached its height under Emperor Coltonus the Great. Rovo itself is never visited as a location in a story; it is background history that explains Toscan culture (legions, city-states). Position on this map is a rough guess.

    Santorillos

    Village or town

    A volcanic island in the Constant Sea, close to Byzantos, with a nearly impregnable fortress on the rim of an extinct volcanic crater. Hideout of the corsair Myrgos and his ship the Vulture. Here the Herons had to protect the young heir against the pirates operating out of the crater.

    The Caldera

    Scorpion Mountain

    Arrida

    The rocky sanctuary of the Scorpion cult: assassins who kill with a poison only they know the antidote for. After Cassandra was struck by one of their killers, the Herons had to seek the cult out in its own mountain.

    Scorpion Mountain

    Seacliff

    Castle · Araluen

    A small island fief off the east coast: Will's first posting as a fully-fledged Ranger. Quiet, remote and — to his frustration — with almost no work for a Ranger.

    The Sorcerer of the North

    Skorghijl

    A bare, misty rocky island with a sheltered crater harbor, where wolfships ride out the autumn storms. Will and Evanlyn spent weeks here aboard Erak's ship Wolfwind.

    The Icebound Land

    Socorro

    Harbour town · Arrida

    An Arridi port town notorious for its slave market. When Tursgud raided a village on the Araluen coast and sold its people here, Hal and the Herons rescued them together with the Ranger Gilan.

    Slaves of Socorro

    Solitary Plains

    Araluen

    An empty, marshy area in the southwest of Araluen.

    Sorato

    Village or town · Toscana

    A valley in northern Toscana, where Will and Maddie set a trap for a Temujai reconnaissance force that has crossed the border. Will brings in Genovesans as crossbowmen to help close the trap.

    Ambush at Sorato

    South Cliffs

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The steep southern coast below the mountains.

    Summer Palace

    Castle · Nihon-Ja

    Emperor Shigeru's summer residence, a lighter and more open building than the winter palace at Ito. Shigeru likes to withdraw here, away from the court; it is precisely this taste for life outside the capital that leaves him exposed when Arisaka strikes. The palace is built of wood and paper, with sliding walls that open onto the gardens — handsome to look at, but impossible to defend against a Senshi army.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja

    Tabork

    Harbour town · Arrida

    A port town on the Arridi coast, a port of call for the Herons on their search for the antidote for Cassandra.

    Scorpion Mountain

    The Fens

    Araluen

    Boggy lowland along the east coast; hard to cross and sparsely settled.

    The Fissure

    Battlefield · Araluen

    The deep chasm that separates Araluen from Morgarath's territory. Here Morgarath secretly had a bridge built to bring his Wargals up behind the Araluen army. Will and Horace burned the bridge down — and were then captured by Skandian raiders.

    The Burning Bridge

    The hunting cabin in the highlands

    Skandia

    High in the mountains above Hallasholm, Will and Evanlyn found an abandoned hunting cabin where they spent the winter. Here Evanlyn helped Will get clean from the numbing drug the Skandians gave the slaves in the great shed.

    The Icebound Land, The Battle for Skandia

    The mountain pass above Hallasholm

    Battlefield · Skandia

    When the Temujai horde invaded Skandia, Skandians and Araluens had to fight side by side for the first time. Halt and Will drilled a corps of archers and Erak chose the ground: a pass where the Temujai couldn't use their cavalry tactics.

    The Battle for Skandia

    The territory of the Bedullin

    Arrida

    The open desert of the Bedullin: nomads with swift horses and a strict code of hospitality. Whoever becomes their sworn friend has allies for life.

    Erak's Ransom

    Thorntree Forest

    Araluen

    The dense forest east of Gorlan, between Hackham Heath and the Plains of Uthal.

    Three Step Pass

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The only way through between Araluen and Morgarath's Plateau: three successive steep steps cut into the rock. Whoever holds it seals off the mountains.

    The Ruins of Gorlan, The Burning Bridge

    Wensley

    Village or town · Araluen

    The village at the foot of Redmont's hill, with an inn, a market, and the fields where the fief's people live. For Will, this is the first inhabited world outside the castle.

    The Ruins of Gorlan, The Burning Bridge

    West Cliffs

    Mountains of Rain and Night

    The cliffs along the Fissure, on the west side of the mountains.

    Western Woodlands

    Araluen

    A vast wooded area in the west of Araluen, between Redmont and the Celtic border.

    Seas and oceans

    Blood Sea

    The sea in the southeast, east of Arrida and the territory of the Tualaghi, south of that of the Temujai. The Assaranyan Canal — dug in ancient times by the Assaranyans — connects it to the Constant Sea. Anyone sailing to Nihon-Ja passes through here and then out onto the Eastern Ocean.

    Constant Sea

    The sea in the south, between Iberion, Toscana and the coast of Arrida. Santorillos lies on this coast.

    Eastern Ocean

    The open ocean east of Nihon-Ja. None of the journeys on this map venture farther than the coast.

    Endless Ocean

    The ocean west of Araluen and Iberion. In Brotherband 6 a storm drives the Heron out onto this ocean, away from any known coast.

    Frozen Sea

    The ice sea north of Skandia. Pack ice drifts here and the water is frozen solid for most of the year; only in summer is there briefly open water.

    Narrow Sea

    The strait between Araluen and the mainland of Gallica and Iberion. Anyone sailing south from the island crosses it first.

    Sea of Rostov

    The inland sea in the east, with Toscana, Aslava, Baralat and the Middle Kingdoms on its shores. According to tradition it connects to the Blood Sea via the Assaranyan Canal.

    Stormwhite Sea

    The inland sea between Sonderland, Skandia, Teutlandt and Gallica. Hallasholm lies on this sea; anyone sailing here from Araluen anchors at Skorghijl along the way.

    Western Ocean

    The open ocean north and west of the known world. None of the journeys on this map venture farther than the coast.

    Travel routes by book

    The Ruins of Gorlan (Ranger's Apprentice 1)

    From Redmont to the ruins of Gorlan: Halt and Will follow the trail of the Kalkara.

    The Burning Bridge (Ranger's Apprentice 2)

    Will, Horace and Evanlyn through Celtica to the Fissure; meanwhile the Araluen army marches to the Plains of Uthal.

    The Icebound Land (Ranger's Apprentice 3)

    Will and Evanlyn are shipped across the Stormwhite Sea, via Skorghijl to Hallasholm; Halt and Horace cross the Narrow Sea to Gallica and travel on horseback to Castle Montsombre.

    The Battle for Skandia (Ranger's Apprentice 4)

    Halt and Horace travel from Castle Montsombre through Teutlandt, over the mountain passes to Hallasholm; then Skandians and Araluens together hold the pass against the Temujai army.

    The Sorcerer of the North (Ranger's Apprentice 5)

    Will leaves his post at Seacliff and travels north as a juggler, to Castle Macindaw.

    The Siege of Macindaw (Ranger's Apprentice 6)

    The siege plays out between Grimsdell Wood and Castle Macindaw; Malcolm's forest becomes the base of operations.

    Erak's Ransom (Ranger's Apprentice 7)

    Across the Narrow Sea, past the west of Iberion, to the coast of Arrida, then into the desert after the Tualaghi.

    The Kings of Clonmel (Ranger's Apprentice 8)

    To Hibernia, where the Outsiders take over kingdom after kingdom: past Mountshannon and Craikennis to Dun Kilty.

    Halt's Peril (Ranger's Apprentice 9)

    The pursuit of Tennyson leads by smuggler ship from Hibernia to the west coast of Picta; from there overland to Malcolm in Grimsdell, where Halt, struck by a poisoned arrow, is treated.

    The Emperor of Nihon-Ja (Ranger's Apprentice 10)

    The longest journey in the series. From the Toscan coast it runs across the Constant Sea, through the Assaranyan Canal and the Blood Sea, then months eastward until Nihon-Ja comes into sight. Halt, Will, Evanlyn and Alyss come ashore at Iwanai, seek out Horace at the summer palace, and travel on through the coastal village of Kawagishi into the snowbound mountains, to the hidden fortress of Ran-Koshi.

    The Lost Stories (Ranger's Apprentice 11)

    Loose stories from the Corps, mostly set around Redmont and Castle Araluen.

    The Royal Ranger (Ranger's Apprentice 12)

    Maddie is posted from Castle Araluen to Will at Redmont; their first case takes them into the south of the fief.

    The Tournament at Gorlan (Early Years 1)

    The young Halt and Crowley travel through Araluen to the tournament at Gorlan, where Morgarath makes his bid for power.

    The Battle of Hackham Heath (Early Years 2)

    The war against Morgarath ends at Hackham Heath, where Will's father Daniel is killed.

    The Red Fox Clan (Ranger's Apprentice 13)

    From Redmont to Castle Araluen, which is threatened by the Red Fox Clan.

    Duel at Araluen (Ranger's Apprentice 14)

    The siege of Castle Araluen: Maddie rides back and forth between the besieged stronghold and the troops outside.

    The Missing Prince (Ranger's Apprentice 15)

    Will and Maddie travel from Redmont and Castle Araluen across to Gallica, disguised as jongleurs. Via La Rivage and Les Sourges they head inland, past Château La Lumière, to Château des Falaises, where Baron Lassigny holds Prince Giles captive — but their rescue attempt fails and they are taken prisoner themselves.

    Escape from Falaise (Ranger's Apprentice 16)

    Halt and Horace cross via Cresthaven to Gallica to aid Will and Maddie, who are held captive in Château des Falaises. Together they manage to escape and free Prince Giles after all; Baron Lassigny falls in the fighting. They then bring the prince to his father at Château La Lumière.

    Arazan's Wolves (Ranger's Apprentice 17)

    Route not yet on the map: the locations from this book still need to be added.

    Ambush at Sorato (Ranger's Apprentice 18)

    Route not yet on the map: the locations from this book still need to be added.

    The Outcasts (Brotherband 1)

    Brotherband training along the Skandian coast at Hallasholm, where the Herons learn to sail their ship.

    The Invaders (Brotherband 2)

    The hunt for Zavac takes the Heron halfway around the west, all the way to Limmat.

    The Hunters (Brotherband 3)

    From Limmat further out onto the Constant Sea, to the pirate haven of Raguza.

    Slaves of Socorro (Brotherband 4)

    As duty ship to Cresthaven in Araluen, and from there after the slave traders to Socorro.

    Scorpion Mountain (Brotherband 5)

    With the antidote for Cassandra at stake: from Araluen to the Arridi coast and into the interior.

    The Ghostfaces (Brotherband 6)

    A storm drives the Heron out onto the Endless Ocean, toward an unknown shore in the west.

    The Caldera (Brotherband 7)

    De bekende wereld

    Alle plaatsen, landen en zeeën op deze kaart, met de boeken waarin ze voorkomen. De kaart zelf is interactief — deze lijst is dezelfde inhoud als gewone tekst.

    Landen en gebieden

    Alpina

    De hoge bergketen die het noorden van het zuiden scheidt. De passen vriezen in de winter dicht; wie dan van Gallica naar het zuiden wil, moet wachten tot het voorjaar of omvaren.

    Araluen

    Het koninkrijk van koning Duncan, verdeeld in vijftig lenen die elk door een baron worden bestuurd. De verdediging rust op drie zuilen: het leger van de barons, de Krijgsscholen die ridders opleiden, en het Korps van de Grijze Jagers — vijftig verkenners met een grijsgroene mantel, een dubbele pijlenkoker en een reputatie die veel groter is dan hun aantal.

    Arrida

    Het woestijnland ten zuiden van de Constante Zee, geregeerd door een emir met wakirs als gouverneurs. In de steden heerst orde en handel; daarbuiten hebben de Bedullin-nomaden en de gevreesde Tualaghi het voor het zeggen. Wie de woestijn in gaat zonder gids of gastvriend, komt er niet uit.

    Aslava

    Uitgestrekt land ten oosten van Teutland en Alpina, dat overloopt in de vlakten waar de Temujai vandaan komen. Wie over land naar het oosten reist, komt hier doorheen.

    Baralat

    Het land aan het oostelijke uiteinde van de Constante Zee, met een grens aan Arrida. Vlak binnen die grens ligt het Amrashin-massief, dicht bij de ingang van het Assaranyaanse kanaal.

    Celtica

    Het bergachtige zuidwesten van het eiland, met een verdrag van wederzijdse bijstand met Araluen. De Celten zijn mijnwerkers en steenhouwers, geen soldaten — toen Morgarath hun dorpen leeghaalde om zijn brug te bouwen, konden ze weinig terugdoen.

    Oostelijke Steppen

    De vlakten in het verre oosten, voorbij Aslava: het land van de Temujai. Zij vechten te paard en met de boog, en trokken van hieruit naar het westen tot ze in Skandia werden gestuit.

    Gallica

    Ten zuiden van de Stormwitte Zee. Er is een koning in naam, maar de macht ligt bij krijgsheren die elk hun eigen kasteel, weg en tol hebben. Voor reizigers is Gallica daarmee het gevaarlijkste land van het westen — en de vaste vluchtroute voor iedereen die Araluen ontvlucht.

    Helleno

    Het land in het noordoosten van de Constante Zee. In de boeken is het geen aaneengesloten kust maar een afgelegen eilandengroep. Veel Hellenen leven van de zeeroverij; ze vallen op door felgekleurde kleren en sieraden die nergens bij elkaar passen. Ze vereren de godin Ariadne.

    Hibernia

    Het eiland ten westen van Araluen, verdeeld over zes koninkrijken die elkaar liever wantrouwen dan samenwerken. Precies die verdeeldheid buitte de sekte van de Buitenstaanders uit, koninkrijk na koninkrijk. Halt werd hier geboren, in Clonmel.

    Iberion

    Het schiereiland in het zuidwesten. Wie vanuit het noorden naar Arrida of de Constante Zee vaart, rondt eerst deze kust.

    Indus

    Het land in het verre oosten, tussen de westelijke koninkrijken en Nihon-Ja. Wie over zee naar het oosten reist, komt langs deze kust.

    Middenrijken

    De rijken tussen Ursali en het oosten. Toen de Temujai voor het eerst naar het westen trokken, werden zij samen met Ursali als eerste onder de voet gelopen.

    Bergen van Nacht en Ontij

    Het onherbergzame gebergte in het zuiden, achter Morgaraths Hoogvlakte. Hierheen trok Morgarath zich terug na zijn nederlaag, met zijn leger van Wargals — beestachtige krijgers die zijn wil rechtstreeks uit zijn gedachten ontvangen. Het gebied is van Araluen gescheiden door De Spleet; de enige doorgang is de Driestappas.

    Nihon-Ja

    Het eilandenrijk in het verre oosten, een half jaar varen van Araluen. Het land wordt geregeerd door keizer Shigeru en verdedigd door de Senshi, een krijgersklasse die vecht met het lange zwaard en zich aan een strenge erecode houdt. Als de Senshi-heer Arisaka de macht grijpt, vlucht de keizer het bergland in, naar het verscholen fort Ran-Koshi — en daar leren de Kikori, houthakkers zonder enige krijgservaring, vechten in gesloten formatie.

    Picta

    Ruig hoogland in het noorden van het eiland, het land van de Scotti-clans. Zij vallen de noordelijke lenen aan zodra de grensvestingen verzwakken — vandaar de gordel van kastelen waarvan Macindaw er één is.

    Skandia

    Het noordelijke zeevaardersland aan de Stormwitte Zee. Skandiërs varen op wolfschepen, vechten met de zware bijl en leefden lang van rooftochten — tot het verdrag met Araluen, gesloten na de gezamenlijke strijd tegen de Temujai, hen tot bondgenoten maakte. Aan het hoofd staat de Oberjarl.

    Sonderland

    Het beboste land ten westen van Skandia, aan de doorvaart tussen de open oceaan en de Stormwitte Zee. Wie van Araluen naar Hallasholm vaart, komt hier langs.

    Teutland

    Het land ten oosten van de Stormwitte Zee, dat Halt en Arnaut doorkruisten op weg naar de Skandische kust.

    Toscana

    Een zuidelijk land van stadstaten, handelaren en marmer, beroemd om zijn legioenen: beroepssoldaten die schild aan schild in gesloten formatie vechten. Die manier van vechten geldt in de hele bekende wereld als voorbeeld.

    Ursali

    Het land midden op het continent, tussen Aslava en Alpina in het westen en de Oostelijke Steppen in het oosten. De Ursali hielden er maar een klein leger op na, en toen de Temujai naar het westen trokken was hun land een van de eerste die viel — samen met de Middenrijken. Wat er van hun nederlaag werd verteld, ging de Temujai vooruit.

    Het open veld waar het leger van Araluen de Wargals opving die uit de bergen kwamen. Toen Morgarath een tweegevecht eiste, nam Arnaut de uitdaging aan — en doodde hem.

    Het dorp onder aan de heuvel van Redmont, met een herberg, een markt en de akkers waar de mensen van het leen wonen. Voor Will is dit de eerste bewoonde wereld buiten het kasteel.

    Zeeklif

    Kasteel · Araluen

    Een klein eilandleen voor de oostkust: Wills eerste standplaats als volwaardig Grijze Jager. Rustig, afgelegen en — tot zijn frustratie — vrijwel zonder werk voor een Jager. In de oorspronkelijke boeken heet het Seacliff.

    De magiër van Macindaw

    Zomerpaleis

    Kasteel · Nihon-Ja

    De zomerresidentie van keizer Shigeru, een lichter en opener gebouw dan het winterpaleis in Ito. Shigeru trekt zich hier graag terug, weg van het hof; juist die voorliefde voor het buitenleven maakt hem kwetsbaar wanneer Arisaka losslaat. Het paleis is gebouwd van hout en papier, met schuifwanden die opengaan naar de tuinen — fraai om te zien, maar niet te verdedigen tegen een Senshi-leger.

    Zeeën en oceanen

    Bloedzee

    De zee in het zuidoosten, ten oosten van Arrida en het gebied van de Tualaghi, ten zuiden van dat van de Temujai. Het Assaranyaanse kanaal — in oude tijden gegraven door de Assaraniërs — verbindt hem met de Constante Zee. Wie naar Nihon-Ja vaart gaat hier doorheen en dan de Oostelijke Oceaan op.

    Constante Zee

    De zee in het zuiden, tussen Iberion, Toscana en de kust van Arrida. Aan deze kust ligt Santorillos.

    Eindeloze Oceaan

    De oceaan ten westen van Araluen en Iberion. In Broederband 6 drijft een storm de Reiger hier de oceaan op, weg van elke bekende kust.

    IJszee

    De ijszee ten noorden van Skandia. Er drijft pakijs en het water is het grootste deel van het jaar dichtgevroren; alleen in de zomer is er kortstondig open vaarwater.

    Nauwe Zee

    De zeestraat tussen Araluen en het vasteland van Gallica en Iberion. Wie van het eiland naar het zuiden vaart, steekt hem als eerste over.

    Oostelijke Oceaan

    De open oceaan ten oosten van Nihon-Ja. Geen van de reizen op deze kaart voert er verder op dan de kust.

    Stormwitte Zee

    De binnenzee tussen Sonderland, Skandia, Teutland en Gallica. Hallasholm ligt aan deze zee; wie er vanuit Araluen heen vaart, gaat onderweg voor anker bij Skorghijl.

    Westelijke Oceaan

    De open oceaan ten noorden en westen van de bekende wereld. Geen van de reizen op deze kaart voert er verder op dan de kust.

    Zee van Rostov

    De binnenzee in het oosten, met Toscana, Aslava, Baralat en de Middenrijken aan zijn oevers. Volgens de overlevering staat hij via het Assaranyaanse kanaal in verbinding met de Bloedzee.

    Reisroutes per boek

    De ruïnes van Gorlan (De Grijze Jager 1)

    Van Redmont naar de ruïnes van Gorlan: Halt en Will volgen het spoor van de Kalkara.

    De brandende brug (De Grijze Jager 2)

    Will, Arnaut en Evanlyn door Celtica naar De Spleet; het leger van Araluen trekt intussen naar de Vlakte van Uthal.

    Het ijzige land (De Grijze Jager 3)

    Will en Evanlyn worden over de Stormwitte Zee gevoerd, via Skorghijl naar Hallasholm; Halt en Arnaut steken de Nauwe Zee over naar Gallica en reizen te paard naar Kasteel Montsombre.

    De strijd om Skandia (De Grijze Jager 4)

    Halt en Arnaut reizen van Kasteel Montsombre door Teutland, over de bergpassen naar Hallasholm; daarna houden Skandiërs en Araluanen samen de bergpas tegen het Temujai-leger.

    De magiër van Macindaw (De Grijze Jager 5)

    Will verlaat zijn post op Zeeklif en reist als jongleur naar het noorden, naar Kasteel Macindaw.

    Het beleg van Macindaw (De Grijze Jager 6)

    Het beleg speelt zich af tussen Grimsdell Woud en Kasteel Macindaw; Malcolms bos wordt de uitvalsbasis.

    Losgeld voor Erak (De Grijze Jager 7)

    Over de Nauwe Zee, ten westen van Iberion langs, naar de kust van Arrida, en dan de woestijn in achter de Tualaghi aan.

    De koning van Clonmel (De Grijze Jager 8)

    Naar Hibernia, waar de Buitenstaanders koninkrijk na koninkrijk innemen: langs Mountshannon en Craikennis naar Dun Kilty.

    Halt in gevaar (De Grijze Jager 9)

    De achtervolging op Tennyson voert per smokkelschip van Hibernia naar de westkust van Picta; vandaar over land naar Malcolm in Grimsdell, waar Halt, geraakt door een vergiftigde pijl, wordt verzorgd.

    De keizer van Nihon-Ja (De Grijze Jager 10)

    De langste reis uit de serie. Vanaf de Toscaanse kust gaat het over de Constante Zee, door het Assaranyaanse kanaal en de Bloedzee, en dan maanden oostwaarts tot Nihon-Ja in zicht komt. Halt, Will, Evanlyn en Alyss gaan bij Iwanai aan land, zoeken Arnaut bij het zomerpaleis en trekken via het kustdorp Kawagishi het besneeuwde bergland in, naar de verscholen vesting Ran-Koshi.

    De verloren verhalen (De Grijze Jager 11)

    De reis begint in Toscana: over de Constante Zee, door het Assaranyaanse kanaal en de Bloedzee naar het verre oosten, naar Iwanai — en dan het bergland in naar Ran-Koshi.

    De koninklijke leerling (De Grijze Jager 12)

    Losse verhalen uit het korps, grotendeels rond Redmont en Kasteel Araluen.

    The Tournament at Gorlan (Vroege Jaren 1)

    Maddie wordt vanaf Kasteel Araluen bij Will in Redmont geplaatst; hun eerste zaak voert het zuiden van het leen in.

    The Battle of Hackham Heath (Vroege Jaren 2)

    De jonge Halt en Crowley trekken door Araluen naar het toernooi van Gorlan, waar Morgarath naar de macht grijpt.

    De Clan van de Rode Vos (De Grijze Jager 13)

    De oorlog tegen Morgarath eindigt op de Heckingse Heide, waar Wills vader Daniel sneuvelt.

    Het duel bij Araluen (De Grijze Jager 14)

    Vanuit Redmont naar Kasteel Araluen, dat door de Rode Vossenclan wordt bedreigd.

    De vermiste prins (De Grijze Jager 15)

    Het beleg van Kasteel Araluen: Maddie rijdt heen en weer tussen de belegerde burcht en de troepen buiten.

    De vlucht uit Falaise (De Grijze Jager 16)

    Will en Maddie steken vanaf Redmont en Kasteel Araluen over naar Gallica, vermomd als jongleurs. Via La Rivage en Les Sourges trekken ze naar het binnenland, langs Château La Lumière, tot aan Château des Falaises, waar baron Lassigny prins Giles gevangen houdt — maar hun poging om hem te bevrijden mislukt en ze worden zelf gevangengenomen.

    De wolven van Arazan (De Grijze Jager 17)

    Halt en Arnaut steken via Cresthaven over naar Gallica om Will en Maddie te hulp te komen, die gevangenzitten in Château des Falaises. Samen weten ze te ontsnappen en alsnog prins Giles te bevrijden; baron Lassigny sneuvelt in het gevecht. Daarna brengen ze de prins naar zijn vader in Château La Lumière.
